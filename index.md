Teacharcha is a space for teachers to share their thoughts, ideas and resources.
It is maintained by [Vicharanashala](https://vicharanashala.ai), the Lab for Education
Design at IIT Ropar.

## What you will find here

Writing and resources from the lab team come first. Resources made by teachers in the
lab's programmes will follow, credited to each teacher and their institution. A
podcast is planned.

## Coming soon

The first pieces are being prepared. In the meantime, you can find the lab's work at
[vicharanashala.ai](https://vicharanashala.ai).
